Silent Invigilator writeup published. The project evolved far beyond the original OpenCV prototype — full Flask/SocketIO backend with JWT auth, role-based dashboards, Flutter mobile client, YOLOv8n object detection with SAHI slicing, MediaPipe FaceMesh with solvePnP head pose estimation, and a spatiotemporal risk accumulator that distinguishes transient movements from sustained malpractice patterns. The temporal scoring engine was the hardest part: tuning the sliding window size and modality weights required a pilot study across 12 participants simulating both normal and suspicious exam behavior.
